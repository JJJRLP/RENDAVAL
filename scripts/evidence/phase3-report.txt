Trustless Work observations
/api-json publishes no maxLength for title or description. A build-only probe
with 10001 characters in EACH returned HTTP 201; it was never signed/submitted.
This establishes an accepted size, not an undocumented absolute maximum.
The real deployment used a 26-character title and 120-character JSON description.
App input caps: issuer 80 characters, series 24; numeric/date fields validated.
Request bodies and contractId response match the spike. SDK 17 XDR accessors use
properties and Uint8Array, not older method/Buffer APIs.
Custom-asset release remains out of scope and known to fail upstream.

No-database concurrency
The issuer account holds the Vault-signed deployment XDR in manageData chunks.
Concurrent serverless requests share one durable transaction instead of relying
on an in-memory lock. Expired/failed transactions are replaced only after Horizon
proves they can no longer commit; confirmed deployments wait for role indexing.
The sample uses 71 chunks plus one count entry: about 36 XLM in issuer reserve.
This is additional testnet account state; no secret keys are stored there.
Payment markers remain on the lender, atomically with payment as requested.
Receipt lookup scans up to 4000 lender transactions for the payment date; this
is sufficient for the demo treasury but needs an indexer for a larger history.

Human click list
1. Open /app on an origin permitted in the Pollar dashboard. Use a fresh company
   account for the complete journey; existing accounts resume their on-chain state.
2. Click Ingresar; complete email/Google login. Wait for account preparation.
3. Click Usar aprobación de prueba, or complete Comenzar verificación and return.
4. In Registre su bono, retain the fictional Bs 6.960 example; click Registrar bono.
5. Check Recibe 500,00 USDC; 8% annual; cost 3,33; total 503,33; 30-day due date.
6. Click Aceptar y depositar garantía. Confirm the deposit when Pollar requests it.
7. Watch the checklist through Dólares enviados. Expect Préstamo activo,
   500 USDC available, USD 1.000 in collateral, and disabled repayment.
8. Reload: the active loan and exact due date must remain. Open Ver detalle técnico
   to inspect registration, creation, deposit and disbursement explorer links.
9. To test resumption, cancel the signature or reload after escrow creation;
   click Continuar con el depósito / Reintentar y continuar. A locked loan resumes
   at desembolso. It must not ask for another deposit after funds are locked.
10. Close the test server when done. No commit, push, branch creation or deployment
    was performed by this task.
